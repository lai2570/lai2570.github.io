兔兔農場：要求刪除資料

App：兔兔農場（Bunny Farm，com.lai.bunnyfarm）　開發者：YUNG-TSAI LAI

如何要求刪除

  1. 打開兔兔農場，到「好友」頁，找到你的好友碼。
  2. 寄信到 r10224214@ntu.edu.tw，主旨寫「兔兔農場 刪除資料」，內文附上你的好友碼。
  3. 我們會在 30 天內刪除，完成後回信通知你。

本 App 不需要註冊帳號，好友碼是我們找到你資料的唯一方式。

會刪除的資料

資料處理方式
Firebase 匿名使用者 ID刪除
暱稱、好友碼、連續種田天數、兔子數量／名字／體型、農場擺設與狀態刪除
你的好友名單，以及你和朋友之間的戳一下、餵食、送禮紀錄刪除

我們不會額外保留任何資料，刪除後無法復原。

存在你手機裡的資料

農場進度、設定等只存在你的手機（以及你自己的 Google 備份）裡，我們看不到。解除安裝 App，或在系統設定中清除本 App 的資料，就會刪除。

天氣功能送到 Open-Meteo 的城市座標只用來即時查詢天氣，我們不會儲存。

更多說明請看隱私權政策。

Bunny Farm: Request Data Deletion

App: Bunny Farm (兔兔農場, com.lai.bunnyfarm)　Developer: YUNG-TSAI LAI

How to request deletion

  1. Open Bunny Farm, go to the Friends page and find your friend code.
  2. Email r10224214@ntu.edu.tw with the subject "Bunny Farm data deletion" and include your friend code.
  3. We delete your data within 30 days and reply when it's done.

The App has no accounts; your friend code is the only way we can find your data.

Data that is deleted

DataHandling
Firebase anonymous user IDDeleted
Nickname, friend code, farming streak, number/names/sizes of bunnies, farm layout and statusDeleted
Your friend list, and pokes, feedings and gifts between you and friendsDeleted

We keep no additional copies. Deletion cannot be undone.

Data on your phone

Your farm progress and settings are stored only on your device (and your own Google backup); we can't see them. Uninstall the App or clear its data in system settings to delete them.

City coordinates sent to Open-Meteo for weather are used only for the live request and are not stored by us.

See the Privacy Policy for details.
