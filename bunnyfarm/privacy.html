兔兔農場 隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 11 日

本政策適用於 Android 應用程式「兔兔農場」（Bunny Farm，套件名稱 com.lai.bunnyfarm，以下稱「本 App」），開發者為 YUNG-TSAI LAI（以下稱「我們」）。本 App 沒有廣告、沒有分析追蹤工具，也不需要註冊帳號。

1. 存在你手機裡的資料

你的農場（兔子、食物、傢俱、種田紀錄、設定等）都儲存在你自己的手機裡。若你的手機開啟了 Android 的 Google 備份，這些資料會隨系統備份到你自己的 Google 帳號，方便換手機時還原；我們看不到這份備份。

2. 好友功能（Google Firebase）

為了讓你和朋友互相看到農場、戳一下、送食物，本 App 使用 Google Firebase：

  • 匿名身分：第一次連線時，Firebase 會產生一個匿名的使用者 ID。它不包含你的姓名、Email 或電話。
  • 公開的農場資料：你設定的暱稱、好友碼、連續種田天數、兔子數量、兔子的名字和體型、農場的擺設與狀態、是否正在種田。只有知道你好友碼的人才能找到你。
  • 互動紀錄：朋友之間的戳一下、餵食、送禮紀錄（誰、什麼時候），收到或過期後就會刪除。

這些資料存放在 Google Firebase（Cloud Firestore，位於台灣 asia-east1 區域）。Google 依其隱私權政策和 Firebase 隱私說明處理資料。

3. 天氣功能（Open-Meteo）

若你在設定中選擇城市，本 App 會把你搜尋的城市名稱和該城市的經緯度送到 Open-Meteo 取得天氣，讓農場跟著下雨或下雪。本 App 不會讀取你手機的 GPS 定位，也不需要定位權限。

4. 付款（Google Play）

付費解鎖和支持開發者都透過 Google Play 付款處理。我們不會收到你的信用卡或付款資料，只會收到「是否已購買」的結果。

5. 通知與鬧鐘

本 App 會在種田結束、睡前提醒時發送通知。這些都在你的手機上處理，不會傳送任何資料。

6. 我們不會做的事

  • 不販售、不出租你的資料。
  • 不放廣告，不使用廣告 ID，不做行為追蹤。
  • 不讀取你的通訊錄、相片、定位、或其他 App 的使用情形。

7. 資料保存與刪除

  • 手機上的資料：解除安裝本 App 或在系統設定中清除資料，就會刪除。
  • Firebase 上的好友資料：保存到你要求刪除為止。請寄信到 lai2570@gmail.com，附上你的好友碼（在 App 的好友頁可以看到），我們會在 30 天內刪除你的匿名身分和所有相關資料。

8. 兒童隱私

本 App 不是專為 13 歲以下兒童設計，我們也不會刻意收集兒童的個人資料。若你是家長，發現孩子提供了個人資料（例如以真實姓名當暱稱），請來信，我們會盡快刪除。

9. 資料安全

本 App 與伺服器之間的連線全部使用 HTTPS 加密。Firebase 的存取規則只允許每個使用者修改自己的資料；朋友只能留下戳一下、餵食和禮物紀錄。

10. 政策變更

若本政策有變更，我們會更新此頁面並修改上方的生效日期。重大變更會在 App 內告知。

11. 聯絡我們

對隱私有任何問題，請寄信到 lai2570@gmail.com。

Bunny Farm Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

This policy applies to the Android app "Bunny Farm" (package com.lai.bunnyfarm, the "App"), developed by YUNG-TSAI LAI ("we"). The App has no ads, no analytics or tracking tools, and does not require you to create an account.

1. Data stored on your phone

Your farm (bunnies, food, furniture, farming history, settings) is stored on your own device. If Android's Google backup is turned on, this data is backed up to your own Google account so it can be restored on a new phone. We cannot see that backup.

2. Friends feature (Google Firebase)

So you and your friends can visit each other's farms, poke, and send food, the App uses Google Firebase:

  • Anonymous identity: on first connection, Firebase creates an anonymous user ID. It contains no name, email, or phone number.
  • Public farm data: the nickname you choose, your friend code, farming streak, number of bunnies, bunny names and sizes, your farm layout and status, and whether you are currently farming. Only people who know your friend code can find you.
  • Interactions: pokes, feedings and gifts between friends (who and when), deleted once received or expired.

This data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, asia-east1 region, Taiwan). Google processes it under its Privacy Policy and the Firebase privacy information.

3. Weather (Open-Meteo)

If you choose a city in Settings, the App sends the city name you search for and that city's coordinates to Open-Meteo to get the weather, so it can rain or snow on your farm. The App never reads your phone's GPS location and does not request location permission.

4. Payments (Google Play)

The paid unlock and developer support are processed by Google Play. We never receive your card or payment details, only whether a purchase was made.

5. Notifications and alarms

The App shows notifications when farming ends and for the bedtime reminder. These are handled on your device and send no data.

6. What we don't do

  • We do not sell or rent your data.
  • No ads, no advertising ID, no behavioral tracking.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, location, or other apps' usage.

7. Retention and deletion

  • On-device data: deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data in system settings.
  • Friends data on Firebase: kept until you ask us to delete it. Email lai2570@gmail.com with your friend code (shown on the Friends page) and we will delete your anonymous identity and all related data within 30 days.

8. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided personal information (for example, a real name as a nickname), please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Security

All connections between the App and servers use HTTPS. Firebase access rules only allow each user to change their own data; friends can only leave pokes, feedings and gifts.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will be announced in the App.

11. Contact

For any privacy questions, email lai2570@gmail.com.
